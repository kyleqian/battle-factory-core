## New scenarios

Template: `mechanic-poison-fang-{player,opponent}-zubat-*.json` with lead carrier swapped, all four
moves = the move (mechanicLab `overrides.parties`, three full legal Pokémon per side, others
unchanged, no injected transient state). Generated by `allocatorNetZeroExtStage.py`. Carriers are
Factory-pool species carrying the move in `battle_frontier_mons.h`: Twineedle Beedrill (15), Bite
Carvanha (330), Hyper Fang / Super Fang Raticate (20), Shadow Punch Haunter (93); the opponent
orientation puts the carrier on the opponent side vs Onix. The first audit's fixtures for these
ids reused the Poison Fang scenario (move never activates); here each activation sequence shows
the move id itself. (Genders: the validator rejects "male" for some ids — Carvanha 318 was a wrong
id guess, fixed to 330.)

Fury Swipes: the original manifest tagged both Fury Swipes scenarios "player" (filename regex hit
the first `player`). `mechanic-fury-swipes-player-onix-opponent-aipom.json` has Aipom on the
opponent side, so the opponent orientation was in fact audited by the original run; the extension
re-measures it explicitly labelled `opponent` (move 154 activations at 87 and 205).
