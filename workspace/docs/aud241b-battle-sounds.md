# aud241b: effectiveness SEs, SE_FAINT, faint cries and the low-HP SE loop (S11 audio step 5, second slice)

Write-only emitters on the Factory singles path (generated orchestrator route). Nothing here changes engine state, RNG, kernel
FactoryEvents, frame hashes or pixels (`tests/aud241bBattleSounds.test.ts` compares the full serialized state with and without a sink,
`tests/audioSinkNeutral.test.ts` the per-frame hashes). The kernel `play-se` event is unchanged; the emitter reads it.

`beginScreenSoundTick` resets the loop flag when a run restarts. Only effective events are emitted; the ROM's no-op stops (every
`HandleLowHpMusicChange` with the loop not playing, 12-16 per window) and the `DestroyAnimSoundTask` volume-256 duck (a move-animation
sound, aud242) are `noop: true` in the fixtures and ignored by the test.

The faint cry's duck restore (`Task_DuckBGMForPokemonCry`) comes `FAINT_CRY_RESTORE_FRAMES[species]` ticks after the cry
(`src/data/faintCryFrames.ts`, generated by `tools/audio/genFaintCryTable.py`): a linear fit `ceil(1.205 t + 0.91)` of the cry sample's
native duration to 15 ROM step-mode windows, exact on all 15 (14 `faint-cry-length-N.json` plus the Rattata of the faint windows;
labs `tools/battle-lab/aud241b-faint-cry-length-N.json`). The other 361 species are a fit, not a measurement.

## Flow mismatches (recorded, not fixed)

1. **Second attack of a turn is 1 tick early** (lowhp ON / OFF: SE 13 at 662 / 275 comes at 661 / 274). Presentation timeline,
   not the emitter; the emitter keeps the ROM's offset from the command's first tick.
2. **Super effective:** the engine's "It's super effective!" print is 5 ticks short of the ROM's, so the faint cry and SE_FAINT after it
   are 5 early (ON). In Scene OFF the Karate Chop hit itself is also 1 tick early (and the faint sounds 6).
3. **Small HP drains:** `hp` commands have a 28-tick floor (`HP_TIMING.minimumFrames`, `lsd194` documents it); the ROM drains the last
   8 pixels (11 -> 0) in 12 ticks, so after a faint from a red bar the faint, SE_FAINT, cry and the loop's stop are 16 ticks late.
4. Mach Punch (priority) was tried first: its SE is 3 ticks early in the engine (move-animation length is aud242 territory), so the
   super-effective fixtures use Karate Chop.
5. The faint restore delay for the 361 unmeasured species is a fit.

## Not covered (unmodelled or unverified)

- **Low-HP at the switch-in** (`SwitchIn_HandleSoundAndEnd`, `battle_controller_player.c:1093/1138`, after the send-out cry's restore):
  `lowHpSong` survives a voluntary switch-out, so a green mon switched in while the loop plays gets a real `stop 90`, and a red mon
  switched in gets `se 90`. No ROM window; **not emitted**.
- **Victory / quit stops** (`battle_main.c:4975/4985/5247` `BattleStopLowHpSound`): a real stop when the loop is playing at the win.
  Left to aud241c (victory music is on the same frame). The emitter's loop flag is not cleared there.
- The intro's HandleLowHpMusicChange (battle start with a red player mon), the exp-gain level-up site (`battle_script_commands.c:3454`,
  Factory has no experience) and the PP / status SetMonData calls that cannot change the bar are not emitted.
- Opponent HP commands never call it (only the player controller does). Turn-end drains (poison, Leech Seed, weather) use the same
  `hp` command and the same offsets; no ROM window checks them.
- The canonical-recorded route: the emitter is wired for the orchestrator's `battleResolutionPresentation` only (as aud241a's
  mid-battle switch is).
- Double battles, other trainers' faint cries (`CRY_MODE_FAINT` pan is per side).
