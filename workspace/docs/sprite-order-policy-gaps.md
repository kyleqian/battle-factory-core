## 0. Answers

**1. Stat command ahead of the body.** The leading `stat` presentation command is **not a graphics event**. It is the `stat-stage-change` semantic event of opcode `B_SCR_OP_STATBUFFCHANGE`
(event sequence 2 after `attackstring` 0 / `ppreduce` 1), turned into a HUD/state command by `battleResolutionPresentation.ts:3436-3459` (stat banner + stage state, `STAT_TIMING`; pushed only when `from !== to`).
The sprite work is a *different* event two sequence numbers later: `MOVE_ANIMATION` (`B_SCR_OP_ATTACKANIMATION`, seq 3) then `animation STATS_CHANGE` (`B_SCR_OP_PLAYANIMATION`, seq 4; argument = `B_ANIM_STATS_CHANGE` arg).
This is the generic shape of both stat scripts (`data/battle_scripts_1.s`: StatUp `statbuffchange` :494, `attackanimation` :499, `setgraphicalstatchangevalues`/`playanimation B_ANIM_STATS_CHANGE` :502-503; StatDown :540 / :546-549); **31 move rows** (15 StatUp + 16 StatDown, incl. Double Team, Feather Dance, Fake Tears, Growl, Leer, Swords Dance, ...; `fixtures/sprite-order-policy-gaps/stat-moves.json`) share it, multi-stat moves have their own scripts.
ROM order (10 stat-move scenarios: Double Team x2, Feather Dance x2, Fake Tears on 3 species pairs x 2 sides, `order-evidence.json`): commit destroy (`EndBounceEffect`) -> **allocator silent** until `LaunchBattleAnimation(gBattleAnims_Moves)` (0 allocator calls in all 10 first-body-of-turn cases) -> move body -> body's last create/destroy -> only then `TryHandleLaunchBattleTableAnimation(General, 1, arg)` (the stat copy sprite; 10/10 launch after the last body call; args 21 = +1 evasion, 46 = -2 Atk, 50 = -2 SpDef).
**Mapping (validated): `stat-stage-change` -> no order transform; `MOVE_ANIMATION` -> body plan; `animation STATS_CHANGE` -> `stat-animation` plan (class 1: `C[f];D[n0]`, class 2: `C[f]` + carried destroy), in event-sequence order.**
Pure-transform replay of the 10 ROM streams (29 edges: body edges and stat-animation edges): with the production body table 20 known / **20 exact / 0 mismatch** (the other 9 edges are after Fake Tears, which has no production body plan, see Q3b); with the Fake Tears relative-y plan **29 known / 29 exact / 0 mismatch**.
Controls: treating the leading `stat` command as invalidating -> 2/29 known; applying the stat plan *before* the body -> 21 of 29 mismatches. So order, not just presence, matters.
**The reported "invalidates at seq 2" no longer holds at HEAD.** `docs/next-adapters-prep7.md` §0 finding 1 (0/24 Double Team, 0/2 Feather Dance known) was measured on a branch that predates the event-driven host (`7748aca15` is not an ancestor of `b36f9f944`). At HEAD `orderEventOf` maps `stat-stage-change` to `{}` (`liveSpriteOrderHost.ts:82-113`, falls through `INVALIDATING_EVENTS`), and the orchestrator branch at `factoryRoundBattleOrchestrator.ts:2188-2192` does not list `stat`. Engine run (`engine-evidence.json`): the 3 first-body routes of `prep7-opponent-routes-0.json` (seeds 329, 554 Double Team; 28 Fake Tears) lend a **known** order now (3/3, prep7 scan 0/3); all 9 stat routes' first request is known. What is genuinely missing is only **after** the body for moves without a production plan (Fake Tears: `[true, false]` on seeds 28/513).

**2. Intimidate-class battle start.** Engine (`engine-evidence.json` `battleStart`): `initializeFreshFactoryBattle` returns `nextEventSequence` 3 with events `0 stat-stage-change`, `1 animation STATS_CHANGE arguments ["22"]`, `2 message STRINGID_PKMNCUTSATTACKWITH` (`factoryBattleStart.ts:464-487`, `runEntryScript("BattleScript_IntimidateActivatesEnd3")`; both leads Intimidate -> 6 events, two animations; no Intimidate -> 0). Argument 22 equals the ROM's `[0,0,0,1,22]`.
Today the orchestrator seeds `invalidateSpriteOrderState` whenever `nextEventSequence !== 0` (`factoryRoundBattleOrchestrator.ts:1088-1093`).
**Yes, the seed can apply the stat transform.** Needed: the ROM entry state *before the first prompt* (`BATTLE_LAB_ORDINARY_SINGLES_ENTRY_WINDOWS` minus its last two windows, i.e. the prompt create/destroy; `knownEntrySpriteOrderState` includes the prompt, which is idempotent at that fixed point: `[4,3,21,..]`). Pre-prompt F = `[4,21,20,5,19,18,...,9,7,3,54,...]` (slot 3 sits between 7 and 54), identical in all 12 scenarios and equal to the observed F at the Intimidate edge.
Replay on the 12 corpus scenarios (Hitmontop x6, Ekans x2, Masquerain x2, Howl-alternate x2), `replay-receipt.json` `q2`: pre-prompt seed -> `stat-animation(22)` -> `action-prompt`+`actions-committed`: Intimidate edge **12/12 exact**, first real move edge **12/12 exact** (`[3,4,21,20,5,19,...]`), **0 mismatch** (24/24). Control (seed = the existing post-prompt constant, i.e. ignoring the animation): first move edge 0/12 exact (12 mismatches) - the reason an unmodelled Intimidate must invalidate today.
Limits: exactly **one** `STATS_CHANGE` animation and only message/ability/stat events; both leads Intimidate (two animations, not in the corpus), a prevented Intimidate (Clear Body etc., pause + message, no animation), Trace, weather/form hooks, White Herb entries stay `invalid`. The allocator host's `firstMove` seed (`createKnownEntrySpriteAllocatorProvider`) is separate; the stat copy is occupancy-net-zero (windows `C[3]; D[3]`) but that host was not validated here (open question 2).

**3. Endeavor (283).** Probed 5 lab scenarios (Diglett->Chansey both sides, Onix->Pidgeotto player, Dragonair->Chansey both sides; `scenarios/endeavor-*`, A/B identical) plus the 3 real-route bodies (frames 2992, 3938, 6006 of the Factory battle-1 traces; the opponent Endeavor at 2992 is the first invalidation of all five canonical traces).
Plan (`sprite-order-body-plans-endeavor.json`, ext format): `Cffff;Cf;D1,3,0,2;Cffff;D4;D6,8,5,7;Cffff;Cf;D10,12,9,11;Cffff;D13;D15,17,14,16` - four bursts of the four sweat droplets of `AnimTask_SquishAndSweatDroplets` (one extra sprite per burst pair), no survivors; **both sides the same**.
The stock classifier says NEEDS-KEYS (4 destroy groups per body: equal priority word, different y). **But the y spread inside each group is a constant 6** (`CreateSweatDroplets`, `battle_anim_effects_3.c:3828-3843`: `yCoords[i & 1] = baseY + yOffset (+6)`); absolute y depends on the *side* (player 96/90 then 56/50; opponent 56/50 then 16/10), not the species (Diglett, Onix, Dragonair give identical ys). The destroy order `[4,7,3,5]` is the array order of (equal word, y descending) and is therefore species-independent: observed in 5 lab bodies x 4 groups (20/20) and in the 3 real-route bodies x 4 groups (12/12). Verdict: **EXACT-PLAN under the relative-y-static rule** (NEEDS-KEYS only under the old absolute-key rule).
Replay: lab 10 edges: without the plan 7 known, **with it 10/10 exact, 0 mismatch**. **Strict real-route replay (5 traces), with Endeavor added:** move edges known 15 -> **44** (3->10, 3->10, 3->4, 3->10, 3->10), anim edges known 5 -> 9; all known edges exact (**0 mismatches, 0 occupancy mismatches** by construction of the plan: net-zero), the first invalidation moves from frame 2992 (`283:opponent`) to the next uncovered unit: 6519 `RunTurnActionsFunctions` (4 traces) / 3336 `HandleTurnActionSelectionState` (dragonbreath trace). Combined edges known per trace 4/4/4/4/4 -> **12/12/5/12/12** of 38/31/5/13/31.
**3b. Fake Tears (313)** is the same class: the committed table has NEEDS-KEYS for the same reason (equal word, y 112/104, 62/54, 30/22, 38/30: constant 8 spread). ## 2. Open questions

1. Fake Tears relative-y rule rests on 5 bodies x 3 absolute-y fingerprints and the observed 8-pixel spread, not on a source reading of the Fake Tears tear sprites (Endeavor does have the source citation). One more probe geometry per side would harden it; the replay is 0-mismatch on all of them.
2. Allocator host seed for Intimidate battles (`createKnownEntrySpriteAllocatorProvider("ordinary-factory").firstMove`): occupancy is net-zero after the stat copy (`C[3]; D[3]`), but no consumer-level validation was run.
3. Both-lead Intimidate (two stat animations), Intimidate on a +/-6-clamped target (no animation, only message) and Intimidate-prevented are unobserved in the ROM; the proposal invalidates them.
4. Real-route replay now stops at `RunTurnActionsFunctions` uncovered units (frame 6519) / `HandleTurnActionSelectionState` (3336): next coverage targets are those uncovered clusters, not body plans (see `docs/sprite-order-real-route.md` §4).
5. `en2-opponent` (Onix Endeavor vs Pidgeotto) never produced an Endeavor body (the lab Endeavor failed on HP), so the Endeavor evidence is 5 bodies (3 player-side, 2 opponent-side), not 6.
