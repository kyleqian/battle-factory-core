# fsn1: no sound loses its start to its first use

Kyle's 2026-11-22 live battle (site, Battle Scene ON, sound on; trace `_playtest-20261122/kyle1122b.json`, outside the repo): foe
Wartortle's first Bite "sounded faint", and foe Minun's Salac Berry "didn't make a sound when the speed went up". Both were the first use of
their files in the page session. The player (`src/audio/player.ts`, aud245b) fetched every file but the start-up set on first use; when the
file arrived late, `play` joined the sound where it would be by then (the attack cut: the faint Bite, SE_M_BITE at 17523) or dropped it when
it was already over (the berry's SE_SHINY at 22170 and its neighbours SE_M_TAKE_DOWN x3 / SE_M_MORNING_SUN / SE_M_STAT_INCREASE). The same
held for every species' first cry and every move's first sound. Two fixes, both in the player and both one way (nothing goes back into the
session; ticks, traces and digests are unchanged).

## 1. The battle prefetch

- `src/audio/battleSoundNeeds.ts` (main chunk, a pure read of the state): `BattleSoundNeeds.update(state)` builds a request from the round's
  rules as soon as the round controller names the next opponent (before the Battle Room walk: frame 14985 for battle 2 of Kyle's trace, its
  first cry at 15860): both parties' members (side, species, ability, moves; `playerKernelParty`, `currentOpponent.rentalRecords[].member`),
  the battle music (`factoryBattleBgm`), the scene, and any move the presentation names that no member knows (a called move: Metronome,
  Mirror Move ...; `presentedMoveIds`). It returns true only when the request changed, and only while a battle is coming or under way
  (rules phase `presentingBattleStart` / `battleActive`: after a win the rules name the beaten opponent until the next is built, so a
  reload into the swap screen fetches nothing for a battle that is over).
- `src/lib.ts` calls it after every live tick, at the end of a resume and after `advance`, and hands a changed request to
  `AudioPlayer.prefetch` (a player created later gets the current one). A throw there is caught: sounds then load on first use.
- `AudioPlayer.prefetch(request)` keeps the latest request; once audio is live it queues (4 parallel fetches, after the unchanged start-up
  set) the send-out cries, each member's move SEs at its own side's pans (Scene OFF: only Transform / Substitute, which animate there), the
  cry-task recordings of Roar / Howl / Hyper Voice users, the abilities' bodies, a called move's SEs (both sides), the common battle SEs, and
  the battle music last. Each SE resolves through `sfxSound`, the recording `startSong` will play. Loaded or queued files are skipped.
  Counters: `battleRequests`, `battlePrefetched` (part of `prefetched`).

### The table: `src/assets/gen/battle-sounds.json`

Generated by `tools/bundle/battleSoundTable.ts` (`--write`, `--check`) from the table the engine plays move sounds from
(`src/data/gen/anim-sound-timing.json`, extracted from the animation scripts) through the engine's own `moveAnimOpRows` (the pan law):

- `moves`: per move and attacker side, every SE start of every variant the entry can select (every turn / side slot and every grid: Rollout
  counter, Seismic Toss damage, healing flag, elevation, top Y, pic Y x HP quarter), against the targets the kernel gives it (the user for
  MOVE_TARGET_USER, the foe otherwise), plus the General_* bodies the move leads to (trap turns, weather turns, Leech Seed drain, Wish,
  Future Sight, Curse, Substitute ...). 344 moves, 22 KB, in the lazy `audio-data` chunk with the manifest (`vite.config.ts`).
- `cryTasks`, `bothScenes`, `abilities` (Drizzle / Drought / Sand Stream weather, Forecast).
- `common` (both scenes): the effectiveness hits and SE_FAINT at both pans, SE_LOW_HEALTH, the ball and tray SEs, stat up / down at both
  sides' pans. `commonSceneOn`: the status conditions, the held-item body (SE_M_TAKE_DOWN, SE_M_MORNING_SUN, SE_SHINY; moveAnimSounds.ts
  `HELD_ITEM_EFFECT_LATER_SE_OPS`, now exported) and Focus Band.

Kept complete by `tests/fsn1FirstUseSounds.test.ts`: the committed file must equal a fresh generation; every Factory move (Level 50 sets
110..849 and Open Level sets 372..881) is walked through the engine's `variantOf` with every input class and `moveAnimOpRows`, and any SE
start its entry does not list fails the test; and seeded Scene ON / OFF sessions (the soak's random player) play every battle SE and cry from
a file the request had already loaded (`late` 0). Wider runs (`.integration/fsn1/e2e.mjs`, `censusCoverage.mjs`; not committed): 24 Scene ON seeds x 60,000 frames (22,915 battle SE /
cry events) and 8 Scene OFF seeds (4,856) had no late voice; the lsd194 census seeds 200..219 put every move-window SE in its move's entry,
the common lists or (5 rows) a General body another move of that battle lists. Outside battles the only first use left in those runs was
SE_SAVE after a lost round.

Not fetched ahead (the late-start rule below plays them whole when they are at most 0.25 s late): a Magic Coat bounce or a Snatch plays a
move at the other side's pans; a summary-screen cry in the draft or the swap; SE_SAVE. The victory music and the after-battle BGM are not
prefetched either (a loss would waste them) and keep the catch-up rule.

## 2. The late-start rule

`player.ts` `lateStart`: when a voice's file arrives after the voice should have started, a **short** sound (a one-shot SE or cry up to
`LATE_FROM_START_MAX_LENGTH_S` = 2 s: 90% of the SE recordings, 98% of the cries) that is at most `LATE_FROM_START_S` = **0.25 s** late
moves whole to the arrival time (start, end and its m4a player's busy time shift), so it plays from file position 0, that much late.
Why 0.25 s: it is the slack the clock already treats as on time (`timeFor` keeps its anchor while an event lands up to latency + 0.25 s
ahead), a sound that late still reads as belonging to its picture (the hit flash, the stat arrows), and it covers a normal cold fetch +
decode of a 5-15 KB file. Later than that, long sounds (Sing, Earthquake, Moonlight ...), the two looping SEs, fanfares and BGM keep the
catch-up rule (join part-way, drop when over): their stop / duck-restore events are timed to the picture. Only a voice's own start is
moved, never a restore, a resume or audio coming on. A file that was ready plays exactly as before (`realize`'s ready branch is unchanged).
Counters: `late` (unchanged meaning: the file was not ready at the voice's start) = `lateDelayed` + `lateClipped` + `lateLost` + the ones
whose file still arrived before their onset (played on time).
