## §1. The three entry commands

`Cmd_seteffectwithchance` (`battle_script_commands.c:2908-2939`) is a three-way `if / else if / else` whose **first two arms both call `SetMoveEffect(FALSE, …)`** and whose tail unconditionally clears two bytes. `Cmd_seteffectprimary` (`:2941-2944`) and `Cmd_seteffectsecondary` (`:2946-2949`) are one-line wrappers with **no** tail clear of their own.

Port: `src/engine/battleScriptHitChainOpcode.ts:4016` `createSeteffectwithchanceOpcodeHandler`, `:4070` `createSeteffectprimaryOpcodeHandler`, `:4095` `createSeteffectsecondaryOpcodeHandler`, with the shared tail helper `finalizeSeteffectwithchance` at `:3993`.
