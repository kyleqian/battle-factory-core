# lbr-d2b: ROM compare windows for the 7-win Lobby return (Battle Scene OFF)

Evidence-only step. `src/` is untouched; engine constants that disagree with the ROM are listed under "Residuals" with the measured ROM value,
the engine value and file:line.

## Method

* The oracle cannot poke state, so the won terminal is reached by playing a real final battle: `tools/battle-lab/lbrd2b-seventh-win-off.json`
  (copy of `scratch-win-go-on-style-commentary-probe.json` with `battleNum: 6`, `trainerId: 110`; the lab stages streak = 6 and the ROM wins the
  7th battle) on the `battle-scene-off` oracle.
* `F0 = 5113` is the oracle frame at which `gMain.callback2` becomes `CB2_LoadMap` into the Lobby after the won battle (read from the lab capture
  state). Probe relative tick `r` = oracle frame `F0 + r`. The app stage `seventh-win-terminal-off` (`tools/compare/appTrace.ts`) drives a real
  orchestrator round to completion with the carry from `createInitialFactoryCarry`, so app terminal age 0 = frame `F0` and the first tick after the
  stage is age 1.
* Input schedule (rel ticks; A presses before the menu are 1-frame): 300, 1100, 2200, 3500, 4400; then
  * NO: A at 4424 (record NO), A at 4465 (farewell dismiss).
  * YES: UP at 4424, A at 4430, A at 4504, A at 4545.
  * attendant: the NO route, then 2-frame A presses at 4480 + 100k (k = 0..14), captured over rel 4467-5882 (door walk and fade included).
* Traces: `tools/compare/traces/lbrd-won-{no,yes,attendant}-off.json`, `oracleProfile: battle-scene-off`, generated by
  `.integration/evidence/lbrd2b/mk.py` (not committed; evidence only).

## Settled open points

* **Farewell glyph +-1 tick.** The farewell clear / first-glyph / last-glyph frames match the ROM exactly in both NO and YES (no diff pixels
  anywhere in the farewell window). The +-1 noted by lbr-d2a is the probe's tick convention (edge = the tick the A is processed), not an engine
  error. `declineInputDelay` 41 and `recordSavedInputDelay` 74 (`src/engine/factoryTerminalPresentation.ts:265-277` region) are consistent with
  the second A landing at edge + 102 in the ROM: both traces match through the dismiss.
* **`recordDisabledFarewellEdgeFromRestore` (166, copied from the loss profile).** NOT measurable on the won route: a won round always shows the
  record question (the record-disabled variant needs a cannot-record save state), so no frame in these traces reads the constant. It stays
  unmeasured; it needs a cant-record staging, which the oracle cannot poke and the lab does not provide. Left as lbr-d1 left it.
* **Won Lobby door walk.** Covered by the attendant trace (frames 10740-10995). It matches apart from the wandering woman NPC (below); walk
  length and fade timing agree with `ATTENDANT_TIMING`.

## Residuals

1. **Record-question wait cursor is 1 tick late in the app (1 px).** ROM arrow first drawn at frame 8892 (rel 3779), app at 8893; every 9-tick
   bounce flip afterwards is 1 tick earlier in the ROM (e.g. 9497 / 9506 vs 9498 / 9507), constant for the whole hold. Only the two captured
   frames in the NO window show it, but it is a constant 1-tick phase offset. The text, box and menu are not affected. Engine:
   `src/engine/factoryTerminalRender.ts:422` (`state.age > inputReadyAge`) and `:423-425` (phase from `state.age - inputReadyAge - 1`), with
   `recordPromptInputDelay: 63` at `src/engine/factoryTerminalPresentation.ts:259` (the loss profile has the same 63 at `:195`). ROM-implied
   arrow start is `promptFirstAge + 62` with the phase counted from there. Not changed (src/ off limits; the loss profile shares the render path,
   so whoever fixes it should re-run `loss-terminal-record-cursor-off`).
2. **Saving -> saved transition is 2-3 ticks early in the app (10689-10713).** In the ROM the "SAVING... DON'T TURN OFF THE POWER." text stays
   through frame 10690 and the box is blank for two frames (10691-10692), then "MEMORY saved the game." types from 10693. The app clears at
   10689 (blank for one frame) and types from 10690, so it is 2 ticks early at the clear and 3 ticks early at the first glyph, and stays 3 ticks
   ahead until the line ends (ROM 10714, app 10711). Engine: `ATTENDANT_SCREENS.saving.autoHold: 259` at
   `src/engine/factoryLobbyAttendant.ts:127` (consumed at `:405-407`) and `ATTENDANT_TIMING.messageToMessageFirstGlyph: 5` at `:159`. ROM-implied:
   `autoHold` 261 and a saving -> saved first-glyph lead of 6 (the shared 5 also drives the "ask" screens at `:450` / `:452`, which match, so
   the lead would need to be saving-specific). The 259 was taken from lbr-b captures, none of which spanned this edge (lbrb saveno / saveb /
   savedlgno stop before it); this is the first compare window to cross it, and it is not won-specific.
3. **NPC wander after release (named since lbra / lbrb).** Woman (bbox x 96-125, up to 397 px) after the script releases the field; ROM
   behaviour is RNG-driven. Pixels outside the NPC boxes are identical in those frames.

No other differences were found in the windows above. The retire-typing-pace and happy-f hand-off residuals of lbra / lbrb are outside these
windows.
